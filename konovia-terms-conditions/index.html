2. What Konovia Is

Konovia is a gamified learning app that helps users prepare for driving theory exams (currently the Spanish DGT theory exam, license type B, with support for other license types and countries as they are added). The App provides lessons, practice exams, progress tracking, and related features.

Konovia is a study aid. It does not guarantee that you will pass any official driving theory exam. Exam content, format, and pass criteria are set by the relevant national road traffic authority (e.g., the DGT in Spain) and may change independently of the App.
