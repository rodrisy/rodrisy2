1. What Gradient Is

Gradient builds a mile-by-mile or kilometre-by-kilometre pace plan for a race based on the course's elevation profile and your goal finish time. Gradient's pacing model is a planning tool, not a guarantee — your actual performance on race day depends on many factors outside the app's control, including weather, fitness, fueling, and how you feel that day.

2. No Account Required

Gradient does not require you to create a password-protected account. Onboarding collects your name, email, and optionally your phone number, to personalize your plan and, if you choose, to send you a copy of it. An anonymous device identity is created automatically the first time you share a course, solely to establish which courses you own.
