Gradient

My IB Maths IA, turned into a real app. The IA modelled the relationship between stride frequency, stride length, and pace using regression and calculus — Gradient takes that model off the page and puts it on your wrist and phone, giving runners live pace guidance based on their own data rather than generic training tables. Currently in App Store review.
