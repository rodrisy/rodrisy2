Name Guessing Research

A machine learning experiment to predict a person's cultural or national background from their first name using pattern recognition. Trained a classifier on a multilingual names dataset, using n-gram features and character-level patterns to identify naming conventions across cultures. Surprisingly accurate for common names, and interestingly bad in certain edge cases.

Done ✓ NLP · Twitter · Python

National Discourse & Online Aggression

IRS research analysing how national crises instigate aggressive discourse online, built around a case study of Spain in 2025. I scraped and analysed Twitter/X data across the year's flashpoints, built a sentiment and aggression classifier, and tracked how rhetoric escalated and cooled around each event. The findings suggest a consistent pattern: crises amplify aggression, and it spreads faster than the corrections that follow it.
